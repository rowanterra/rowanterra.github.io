I successfully defended my Ph.D. in Biological Sciences at <a href="https://www.duq.edu/academics/colleges-and-schools/science-and-engineering/academics/departments-and-programs/biological-sciences/index.php">Duquesne University</a> in October 2026 (degree conferral pending). My doctoral research on biogeochemical processes in abandoned mine drainage (AMD) was conducted at Duquesne and <a href="https://edx.netl.doe.gov/sites/geomicrobiology/">NETL-DOE</a> under an <a href="https://orise.orau.gov/index.html">ORISE</a> research fellowship. These projects (1) characterized AMD feedstocks using field and computational methods and (2) explored microbiological mining for critical mineral (CMM) extraction and recovery.

I have a decade of research experience spanning biological, chemical, ecological, and computational sciences. These opportunities have helped me flesh out my preference for multidisciplinary approaches to scientific questions.

Since defending, I have been wrapping up the remaining publications from my dissertation. In the interim, I joined the DOE-NETL HERMES initiative in November 2026 as an ORISE Postdoctoral Research Fellow. In 2027 I plan to spread my wings and leap into industry with a company that supports ongoing client engagements through my company, Terra Cognita LLC. I am excited to broaden my offerings and take on new projects through Terra Cognita once the fellowship ends in early 2027. More details will be posted in the coming weeks.
